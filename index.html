Who we are

Clinicians teaching clinicians

The International Foundation for Sexual Health and HIV Education (IFSHHE) is an independent, volunteer-led organisation. Our faculty are practising UK clinicians who give their time to strengthen sexual health, HIV and hepatitis education where it is needed most.

We work in partnership with local health professionals and organisations. Together we deliver education that fits the local health system, respects local culture and improves care for patients. We also help local educators take the teaching on, so it continues without us.

Designed with local clinicians, not for them.

  • Independent
  • Volunteer-led

What we do

Three things, done well

Everything we do comes back to one aim: more clinicians, more confident, in more places.

01

Education and training

Evidence-based teaching in sexually transmitted infections, HIV, hepatitis and sexual and reproductive health, shaped to the local workforce and the cases they see.

02

Building local capacity

We develop local clinical educators, support service improvement and leave behind materials that teams can keep using.

03

Lasting partnerships

Long-term relationships with hospitals, universities, professional societies and NGOs, so each visit builds on the last.

How we work

With partners, from the first conversation

  1. 1

    Co-design

    We start by listening. Local partners tell us what their clinicians need; we build the programme together.

  2. 2

    Deliver

    Volunteer faculty teach in person: lectures, case discussions and hands-on workshops, in small groups wherever we can.

  3. 3

    Train the trainers

    Local educators co-teach from day one, so the course belongs to them, not to us.

  4. 4

    Sustain

    We stay in touch, share materials, mentor and return when invited.

Our impact

Measured by what lasts

We ask every participant what they learned and what they will change. We check knowledge before and after each course. And we judge ourselves by one thing above all: whether the teaching carries on without us.

  • Co-designed, every time

    Each programme is planned with the partner, not sent from the UK.

  • Taught by local educators

    Local clinicians co-teach from the start and take the course forward.

  • Checked, not assumed

    Participant feedback and knowledge checks shape the next visit.

  • Partnerships that continue

    We keep in touch between visits and return when invited.

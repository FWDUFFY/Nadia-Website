Who we are

The International Foundation for Sexual Health and HIV Education (IFSHHE) is an independent, volunteer-led organisation dedicated to strengthening sexual health, HIV and hepatitis education worldwide.

We work in partnership with local healthcare professionals and organisations to deliver sustainable, culturally relevant education that builds capacity, supports service development and improves patient care.

What we do

Education and training

Delivering evidence-based education and training in sexually transmitted infections, HIV, hepatitis and related areas, tailored to local healthcare systems and workforce needs.

Capacity building

Working with local healthcare professionals and organisations to strengthen knowledge, develop local educators and support sustainable improvements in sexual health, HIV and hepatitis care.

Global partnerships

Building long-term partnerships with local organisations, universities and healthcare institutions to co-design and deliver sustainable education programmes.
